# The Maggakes Estate

A 3D luxury-mansion roleplay game for the browser. You work your way up a 25-level mansion tower by
doing tasks, earning Aura Points, unlocking floors, shopping for clothes, furniture and cars, and
decorating your own bedroom. When you reach the rooftop, **Love Island Mode** unlocks, and the tower
keeps going up to the **24th floor**. Each floor opens 1,000 Total Aura after the last one. You can
also drive out of the car park to the **beach**, the **shopping mall** and **town**.

**Play:** https://maggakes-estate.sw573bfk.workers.dev

## Who can play

**Anyone can make an account** — they pick a username (3–16 letters, numbers or _) and a password.
New accounts wait until an **admin approves** them. Admins see a red badge on the 👥 **Admin** button
and approve or reject from the Accounts page. Every two approved newcomers get a brand-new
**Residence floor** with two bedrooms they can decorate.

- **Admins:** Beau (main admin — only Beau can ban, demote or reset Beau), Keola and Milla.
  Admins can approve/reject sign-ups, give/remove VIP, reset passwords, ban/unban and make other admins.
- **VIPs** can see every account but can't change anything.
- Passwords are scrambled (hashed) — nobody can read them, only reset them.

The 10 original residents keep their bedrooms on the original floor plan:
Phoebe & Serena (Basement), Beau (1st floor guest room), Keola & Jess (4th), Ava & Grace (5th),
Charis & Gabby (6th), Milla (7th).

## How it plays

- **Aura Points.** Each floor has 5 tasks worth 200 Aura each. Every 1,000 *Total Aura* unlocks the
  next floor. *Available Aura* is what you spend in the shop. Spending never takes away Total Aura,
  so you can't lose progress.
- **Basement, 1st floor and car park** are open from the start. Your own home floor is always open so
  you can decorate your room, but its tasks only count once you've unlocked it.
- **Replaying** a task gives +50 Available Aura, at most once every 2 minutes per task (it doesn't
  count towards unlocking floors).
- **Daily challenges** (3 a day, plus 1 more for VIPs) give extra Aura.
- **Shop:** outfits, sunglasses, handbags, jewellery, hats, perfume (sparkle trail), furniture and cars.
  Some items need a certain floor, some are VIP-only.
- **Decorate:** stand in your own bedroom and press *Decorate my room*. Everyone on that floor sees it.
- **Car park:** pick a car, paint and wheels at the garage, then press *Drive*.
- **Love Island Mode:** 5 chats a day, 36 chat topics and 12 Beach Hut questions (no repeats until
  you've seen most of them). Couple up with friends or the islanders, win challenges for Villa Points,
  recouple at the firepit, and reach 1,500 Villa Points while coupled to win the final.

- **🎉 Fun menu (F):** hop in a **mini kart** and drive on any floor, **daily spin wheel**, **party**
  (disco lights + music; dancing earns Aura), **tag** with friends, **fireworks** (buy in the shop),
  your **pet** (adopt a puppy, kitten or bunny at the 2nd floor Pet Room — it follows you everywhere),
  **golden shell** tracker (30 hidden around the mansion, +1,000 Aura for finding them all) and **music**.
- **🏁 Kart race** at the car park on a painted track (kerbs, arrows, start/finish line), with a
  3-2-1 countdown, a yellow arrow over your kart pointing to the next checkpoint, a light beam on
  each checkpoint, **↺ Reset kart** (or **R**) if you get stuck, a lap timer and a best-lap board.
  Plus a **🌊 water slide** on the rooftop.
- **Day & night:** a shared 20-minute day — sunrise, sunset and starry nights for everyone at once.
- **The tower (13th–24th floors):** Aquarium, Fashion Studio, Bakery Café, Spa & Salon, Art
  Studio, Theme Park (carousel and Ferris wheel), Space Observatory, Jungle Conservatory, Candy Land,
  Haunted Floor, Music Festival and Sky Garden, with 5 tasks each and new shop items.
- **The outside world:** at the car park, press *🚗 Road trip* to drive to **Maggakes Beach**
  (swim in the sea), the **Shopping Mall** or **Maggakes Town**, with 5 tasks each. Your car is parked
  there to drive back.
- **Mini-games:** besides sequence, hold, timing, pattern, quiz and collect, tasks now include
  **catch** (move the basket), **memory match**, **rhythm tap**, **spot the odd one out**, **maze**
  and **unscramble**.
- **📜 Quests & story:** 7 characters around the estate (Mayor Maggie in town, Captain Coral at the
  beach, Chef Marco, Madame Glitz, Ranger Rosie, Professor Nova and Jazz) give 15 quests across 8
  chapters. Look for ❗ over their heads. Steps only count what you do after accepting, ✅ means
  you can hand in. Finishing every quest makes you a *Maggakes Legend*. See *⭐ Progress → 📜 Quests*.
- **⚽ Soccer stadium:** drive there on a road trip. Real 3v3 matches: everyone in the stadium plays
  in the same 3-minute match, split into 🩷 Pink and 💙 Blue, with bots filling empty spots (each side
  has a bot keeper). Walk into the ball to dribble, press **E** to shoot (harder while sprinting).
  Win 150, draw 75, lose 40, plus 25 per goal, for the first 10 matches a day. 5 soccer tasks too.
- **Never finished (⭐ Progress tabs):**
  - **🔥 Streak:** claim a reward every day you play. It grows each day in a row (up to +1,050 a day)
    and resets if you miss a day, with big bonuses and badges on days 7, 30, 100 and 365.
  - **📖 Collection:** a book of every outfit, accessory, furniture and car. Rewards and badges at
    10%, 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% (+50,000 Aura).
  - **🐾 Pet levels:** your pet earns XP when you do almost anything and levels up to 50, learning
    tricks (sit, spin, jump, roll over, dance, backflip, sparkle aura) that everyone nearby sees.
  - **🏰 Estate upgrades:** 10 huge buys from 10,000 to 750,000 Aura (about 2.4 million total). Each
    gives a title and +3% Aura from playing, forever.
  - **Tiered achievements:** 25 tracks with tiers I–V (125 more achievements), the last tiers take
    months.
- **⏸️ Pause (Beau only):** in *👥 Admin*, write any message (or tap *Use the $15 message*) and press
  *⏸️ Pause the game*. Everyone except Beau is sent to a paused screen showing the message. Nobody
  else can sign in or sign up while it's paused, and nothing is lost. While paused, *✏️ Update
  message* changes everyone's screen within about 20 seconds, and *▶️ Unpause* brings everyone back
  automatically.
- **💬 Staff Chat:** a private chat for all admins and VIPs (button next to Admin). You're added
  automatically when you're given VIP or admin, and kicked out the moment it's taken away or you're
  banned. The last 100 messages are kept.
- **⭐ Progress (levels, achievements, weekly events).** Every Aura you earn is XP. Levels go up to
  100, and each gives an Aura reward, with badges and free items at milestones. There are 170+
  achievements to claim, and a different themed event every week (Spooky, Fashion, Summer, Winter,
  Grand Prix, Villa Love, Arcade, Pets) with three goals and two shop items you can only buy that
  week. Rewards are only given when you press *Claim*. Your level shows on your name tag and the
  leaderboard.
- **🎨 Make it yours.** Almost everything can be changed:
  - **Wardrobe** has five tabs. *Look*: any colour skin, hair, eyes and lips, plus build, height,
    hairstyle and facial hair. *Clothes*: a shop outfit or 🧩 mix & match (9 tops, 8 bottoms,
    7 shoes) in any colour. *Extras*: accessories. *Saved*: 6 named outfit slots. *Profile*: name-tag
    colour, a badge and a short bio. Walk up to another player and press *👤 profile* to read theirs.
  - **Bedroom:** in *Decorate*, give a piece of furniture any colour and size before placing it.
    *✏️ Edit nearest* lets you recolour, resize, spin, move or put away something already placed.
    *🎨 Walls & floor* sets any wall colour, 11 floor styles and a floor tint, or empties the room's
    built-in furniture so you can decorate it from scratch.
  - **Garage:** any paint colour and any wheel colour for every car, including the Mini Kart.
  - **Pets:** any fur and collar colour.

Controls: WASD / arrows (or the joystick on phones) to move, **Shift** to sprint, **Space** to jump
(🏃 and ⤴️ buttons on phones), drag to turn the camera, scroll / pinch to zoom, **E** to interact,
**Enter** to chat, **1–5** for emotes. Toilets, sinks, showers and baths can all be used.

## Developing

```bash
npm install
npm run build          # build the game into dist/
npm run dev:server     # local server + game on http://127.0.0.1:8787
npm test               # game-rule tests
npm run typecheck
npm run deploy         # build + deploy to Cloudflare
```

- `src/shared/` – game rules used by both sides: floors and tasks, shop, saves, Love Island.
- `src/server/` – Cloudflare Worker + one Durable Object that handles sign-in, saves,
  the leaderboard and live multiplayer.
- `src/client/` – the 3D game (three.js). Floors are laid out in `world/layouts.ts`;
  all furniture is built in code in `engine/props.ts`, so there are no model files.

Open the game with `?debug` and run `__checkZones()` in the console to check that every room,
door and task on every floor can be reached on foot.
